About me

I'm a Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics at Florida Tech, where I also finished my B.S. in 2026, magna cum laude and as an Apollo Honors Scholar. I arrived as an aerospace engineering major and moved to applied mathematics after my first semester, but space has stayed close to my work.

Since 2023 I've been a data scientist in the Neural Transmissions (NETS) Lab, working on machine learning for government and NSF-funded research. In summer 2025 I interned at NASA Goddard on the Magnetospheric Multiscale mission.

I also enjoy teaching. In spring 2026 I taught algebra, chemistry and forensics to home-school students in grades 6–12 at SPARK Innovative Laboratories.
